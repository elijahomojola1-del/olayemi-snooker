The workshop

We look after tables and cues too

Good equipment plays well for years if someone keeps it right. Bring it in, or ask us to come to you.

  • Re-tipping

    A new tip fitted, shaped and ready for chalk while you wait.

  • Re-clothing

    Worn or torn cloth stripped and replaced, bed and cushions.

  • Table setup

    Delivery, assembly and levelling, so the ball runs straight.

  • Cue repairs

    Ferrules, joints, dents and a clean and oil for tired shafts.
